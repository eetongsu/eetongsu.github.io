Research summary

Reinforcement learning can support power system operation and control, but exploration and policy deployment may violate operational constraints. This review organizes methods for incorporating safety into learning and decision-making, including Lagrangian approaches, safety layers, and Lyapunov-based techniques. It discusses applications in security control, real-time operation, and operational planning, as well as benchmarks and implementation challenges. The review is a useful entry point for comparing safety mechanisms and their assumptions; the strength of a safety claim depends on the particular method and setting.

Questions this work addresses

  • Which mechanisms are used to incorporate safety constraints into reinforcement learning?
  • Where have safe RL methods been applied in power systems?
  • What challenges remain for scalable and robust deployment?
