Neural Network Certification Informed Power System Transient Stability Preventive Control with Renewable Energy

Tong Su, Junbo Zhao

IEEE Transactions on Power Systems 40 (6), 5398–5409

Author manuscript (PDF)DOIarXivResearchGateBibTeX

Research summary

A learned transient stability surrogate can accelerate preventive control, but its predictions require additional scrutiny under uncertain operating conditions. This paper combines a deep belief network with transient stability-constrained optimal power flow and uses the α,β-CROWN verifier to inform stability margins. The resulting certification is used within the optimization process to balance security and operating cost. Numerical evaluation uses a modified western South Carolina 500-bus system. The certification applies to the modeled network and specified uncertainty setting; it should be interpreted within those assumptions.

Questions this work addresses

  • How can neural network certification inform transient stability-constrained OPF?
  • How are renewable-generation and load uncertainties addressed?
  • How can a certified surrogate support preventive dispatch?
