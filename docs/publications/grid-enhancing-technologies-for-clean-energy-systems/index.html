Tong Su, Junbo Zhao, Antonio Gomez-Exposito, Yousu Chen, Vladimir Terzija, Jake P. Gentle

Research summary

This review addresses grid-enhancing technologies in the context of clean energy systems. It provides a broad research entry point for readers studying how electricity networks can accommodate the energy transition. The article appeared in the inaugural volume of Nature Reviews Clean Technology.
